# Aashan ERP v5.6.0

## Customer statements and payment reminders
- Customer Statement of Account with customer/date filters, running balance, print/PDF, CSV export and email.
- Payment Reminder action for every unpaid or partially paid invoice.
- Editable Payment Reminder Email and Customer Statement Email templates.
- Mobile-compatible buttons and responsive statement controls.
- Optional SQL creates payment reminder history and inserts default email templates.
- Existing accounting, startup reliability, attachments, PWA and mobile UI retained.
